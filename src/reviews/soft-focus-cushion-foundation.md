I wanted to love this one. For about two hours a day, I do.

## The finish

This is the "your skin but photographed well" effect people talk about. It sits
in a light layer and gives back a soft glow rather than a shine. Under indoor
light it looks lovely, and it doesn't cling to the dry patches on my cheeks.

## The problem

By hour four it starts separating around my nose, and by hour six there's a
visible gap where the product used to be. Setting powder helps a bit; primer
helps more; neither fixes it.

## Would I buy it again?

Not at this price. But I'm keeping the one I have, because for a three-hour
dinner it's genuinely the best thing I own.
