Some products earn a five out of five by being remarkable. This one earns it by
never once being a problem.

## Texture

It's thick in the bottle — you have to squeeze — but it breaks down into
something much lighter the moment it hits skin. I can get dressed about a minute
after applying, which is not true of most things this rich.

## Winter test

I started using it in the coldest part of the year, when the backs of my hands
usually crack. They didn't. That alone is worth the price.

## The only complaint

The cap. It has come off in my bag twice. Store it upright.
