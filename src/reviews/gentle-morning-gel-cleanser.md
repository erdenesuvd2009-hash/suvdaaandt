I've been using this every morning for about seven weeks, which is long enough
for the honeymoon phase to wear off.

## First impressions

The texture is a thin, clear gel — closer to water than to the thick cleansers
I'm used to. It foams a little, but not much, and that turned out to be the
point. My skin doesn't feel stripped afterwards, which is the whole reason I
went looking for a low-pH cleanser in the first place.

## How it behaved over time

Weeks one and two: nothing dramatic. That's a good sign with a cleanser — the
best ones are boring.

By week four the small bumps along my jaw had calmed down noticeably. I don't
want to give a cleanser all the credit, since it's only on my face for thirty
seconds, but nothing else in my routine changed.

## Who I'd recommend it to

If your skin is combination and gets irritated easily, this is worth trying. If
you have properly dry skin, you might still want something creamier.
